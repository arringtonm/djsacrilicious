Sounds

"What kind of music do you spin?"

I've been DJing for over twenty years (!!) and excel at playing a wide variety of music. And, like all other successful DJs, I have found the best approach is to focus on the people in front of me, both reading and guiding the crowd and taking the kind of careful risks that make a night memorable. My sets are diverse and depend on where I'm playing and who I'm playing for.

DJ Sacrilicious on Soundcloud DJ Sacrilicious on Mixcloud

That said, my weekend club sets are largely comprised of R&B, indie dance, hip hop, house, disco, pop throwbacks and probably a few more assorted other genres filling in the remainder. My wedding sets tend to more heavily incorporate motown, soul, and funk — a careful balance of guaranteed floor fillers without getting cheesy. I play a diverse range of genres for the many corporate events I spin and typically keep the music clean per request. I have a few different series of mixtapes, as well, which tend to lean either more house/indie dance/electronic or more R&B.
